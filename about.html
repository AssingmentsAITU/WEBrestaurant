Our story

Our story started with one wok and one question.

Yuzu Kitchen began as a weekend pop-up run by three friends who couldn't agree on sushi or stir-fry, so the menu became both. We still follow the same rule: fresh ingredients, an open kitchen and no rush at the table.

Our promise

What we believe in

  • Rice is cooked fresh every two hours, never held overnight.

  • Every sauce is made in-house, with no bottled substitutes.

  • We buy vegetables from local Astana growers whenever the season allows.

  • Every table gets the same care, whether it's a date night or a solo bowl of ramen.

Meet the kitchen

The person behind the pass

Possuka Nakamode, Head Chef

Trained in Osaka before bringing wok cooking home to Astana. Possuka designs every seasonal menu and still plates the first order of the night herself.

Off the clock, she's usually testing new dipping sauces on the rest of the team and collecting their honest opinions before anything goes on the menu.
