How a visit usually goes

  1. Grab a seat at the counter or a table. No reservation is needed before 6 pm.
  2. Order small plates to share, or a full bowl to yourself.
  3. Ask the sushi counter what's freshest that day. It changes often.
  4. Save room for the matcha dessert. We mean it.
